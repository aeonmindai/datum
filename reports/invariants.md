Generated by `npm run test:invariants` against **real Postgres 18.6 (Debian 18.6-1.pgdg13+2)** in a
container. Nothing in this suite is stubbed: every refusal below came out of the
database, and every reason is the constraint or trigger name Postgres reported.

## Case 1 — no evidence

- expected: **rejected** (invariant 1)
- accepted: `false`

- message, verbatim:

  ```

  ```
- observed:
  - `evidence: null` = `"evidence_required"`
  - `evidence: {}` = `"evidence_required"`
  - `evidence: {source:'   '}` = `"evidence_required"`

### Mutation check

| direction | accepted | reason | sqlstate |
|---|---|---|---|
| guard present | `false` | `evidence_required` | `23514` |
| guard removed | `true` | `—` | `—` |

Expected without the guard: the evidence-free write lands. Observed: `true`.
- `evidence: null` = `"ACCEPTED"`
